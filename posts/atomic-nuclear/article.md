## #atom-structure 01 / Atomic Structure :: 从葡萄干布丁到行星模型 || From Plum Pudding to Planetary Model

原子模型的演进：汤姆孙 → 卢瑟福 → 玻尔。α 粒子散射实验推翻了汤姆孙模型，确立了原子的核式结构。 || Atomic model evolution: Thomson → Rutherford → Bohr. α scattering experiment overturned Thomson's model, establishing nuclear structure.

:::accordion
::panel { open: true }
### 三代模型对比 || Three Model Comparison
| 模型 || Model | 年代 || Year | 核心思想 || Core Idea | 局限 || Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 汤姆孙 || Thomson | 1904 | 正电荷均匀分布，电子嵌入（葡萄干布丁） || Positive charge uniformly distributed, electrons embedded (plum pudding) | 无法解释 α 散射大角度偏转 || Cannot explain large-angle α scattering |
| 卢瑟福 || Rutherford | 1911 | 正电荷集中在极小的核中，电子绕核运动 || Positive charge concentrated in tiny nucleus, electrons orbit | 经典电磁学预测电子会螺旋坠入核 || Classical EM predicts electron spirals into nucleus |
| 玻尔 || Bohr | 1913 | 电子只能在特定轨道运动；跃迁发射/吸收光子 || Electrons only in specific orbits; emit/absorb photons during transitions | 仅适用于氢原子 || Only works for hydrogen |

::panel
### α 粒子散射实验 || α Particle Scattering
1909–1911 年盖革-马斯登实验：α 粒子射向金箔，绝大多数直穿，少数偏转大角度，极少数被反弹。 || 1909–1911 Geiger-Marsden: α particles at gold foil. Most pass through, few deflect at large angles, very few bounce back.

1

**大多直穿 || Most Pass Through**

原子内部大部分是空的（核外空间远大于核）。 || Most of the atom is empty space (nucleus is tiny compared to atom).

2

**少数大角偏转 || Few Large-Angle**

正电荷集中在很小的核中，对 α 粒子产生强库仑斥力。 || Positive charge concentrated in tiny nucleus, producing strong Coulomb repulsion.

3

**极少反弹 || Very Few Bounce**

核的质量远大于 α 粒子（金核 197u vs α 4u），正面碰撞可反弹。 || Nucleus mass ≫ α particle (Au 197u vs α 4u). Head-on collisions can bounce back.

> **💡 原子核尺寸 || 💡 Nuclear Size**
> 原子半径 ~10⁻¹⁰ m，原子核半径 ~10⁻¹⁵ m。核的体积仅占原子的 10⁻¹⁵ 倍。如果原子放大到足球场，核只有一粒沙子大小。 || Atom radius ~10⁻¹⁰ m, nucleus ~10⁻¹⁵ m. Nuclear volume is 10⁻¹⁵ of atomic volume. If atom were a football field, nucleus would be a grain of sand.
:::

## #nucleus 02 / The Nucleus :: 结合能与质能方程 || Binding Energy and E=mc²

原子核由质子和中子组成。核子结合释放巨大能量——质量亏损转化为能量（E=mc²）。 || Nucleus consists of protons and neutrons. Nucleon binding releases tremendous energy — mass defect converts to energy (E=mc²).

:::accordion
::panel { open: true }
### 核的组成 || Nuclear Composition
| 粒子 || Particle | 电荷 || Charge | 质量(u) || Mass(u) | 符号 || Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 质子 || Proton | +e | 1.007 3 | p |
| 中子 || Neutron | 0 | 1.008 7 | n |

核子数 A = 质子数 Z + 中子数 N。同位素：Z 相同但 N 不同的核素。 || Mass number A = proton number Z + neutron number N. Isotopes: same Z, different N.

::panel
### 结合能与质能方程 || Binding Energy & E=mc²
$$
E = mc^2
$$

> **📐 质量亏损 || 📐 Mass Defect**
> 核子结合成原子核时，质量总和 > 原子核质量。差值 Δm 称为质量亏损。 || When nucleons bind into a nucleus, total mass > nuclear mass. The difference Δm is the mass defect.
> $$
> \Delta E = \Delta m\cdot c^2
> $$
> ΔE 即结合能，是把原子核拆成独立核子所需的最小能量。比结合能（ΔE/A）越大，核越稳定。铁-56 附近比结合能最大。 || ΔE is binding energy — minimum energy to disassemble nucleus. Binding energy per nucleon (ΔE/A) higher = more stable. Peak near Fe-56.

- **核聚变 || Nuclear Fusion** — 轻核聚合为较重核（如氢 → 氦），质量亏损释放巨大能量。太阳的能量来源。 || Light nuclei fuse into heavier ones (H → He), mass defect releases enormous energy. Powers the Sun.
- **核裂变 || Nuclear Fission** — 重核分裂为中等核（如铀-235 吸收中子后裂变），释放能量和更多中子（链式反应）。核电站原理。 || Heavy nuclei split into medium ones (e.g., U-235 fission after neutron absorption), releasing energy and more neutrons (chain reaction). Nuclear power.
:::

## #decay 03 / Radioactive Decay :: α、β、γ 射线与半衰期 || α, β, γ Rays and Half-Life

不稳定原子核自发衰变，放出 α、β 或 γ 射线。半衰期描述衰变速度，是统计规律。 || Unstable nuclei spontaneously decay, emitting α, β, or γ rays. Half-life describes decay rate as a statistical law.

:::accordion
::panel { open: true }
### 三种射线对比 || Three Rays Comparison
| 射线 || Ray | 本质 || Nature | 电荷 || Charge | 穿透力 || Penetration | 电离力 || Ionization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| α | 氦核 ⁴He || He-4 nucleus | +2e | 弱（纸挡住） || Weak (paper) | 最强 || Strongest |
| β | 电子 e⁻ || Electron | -e | 中（铝片） || Medium (Al) | 中 || Medium |
| γ | 高能光子 || High-energy photon | 0 | 强（铅/厚混凝土） || Strong (lead/concrete) | 最弱 || Weakest |

> **📐 核反应方程示例 || 📐 Nuclear Reaction Examples**
> α 衰变： || α decay:
> $$
> {}^{238}_{92}\text{U} \rightarrow {}^{234}_{90}\text{Th} + {}^{4}_{2}\text{He}
> $$
> β 衰变（中子转变为质子）： || β decay (neutron converts to proton):
> $$
> {}^{14}_{6}\text{C} \rightarrow {}^{14}_{7}\text{N} + {}^{0}_{-1}\text{e}
> $$
> γ 衰变伴随 α/β 衰变发生，核从激发态跃迁到基态时释放 γ 光子。 || γ decay accompanies α/β decay; nucleus transitions from excited to ground state, emitting γ photon.

::panel
### 半衰期 || Half-Life
$$
N = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/T}
$$

T 为半衰期，经过时间 t 后剩余未衰变原子核数为 N。半衰期是统计规律，对大量原子核成立；对单个原子核无法预测何时衰变。 || T is half-life. After time t, remaining undecayed nuclei = N. Half-life is statistical — valid for large numbers; single nucleus decay timing is unpredictable.

> **💡 碳-14 定年法 || 💡 Carbon-14 Dating**
> ¹⁴C 的半衰期约 5730 年。活体生物中 ¹⁴C/¹²C 比例恒定，死亡后 ¹⁴C 不再补充而持续衰变。测量残余 ¹⁴C 含量即可推算死亡时间，广泛用于考古学。 || ¹⁴C half-life ~5730 years. Living organisms maintain constant ¹⁴C/¹²C ratio; after death, ¹⁴C decays without replenishment. Measuring remaining ¹⁴C gives time since death — widely used in archaeology.
:::

## #practice 04 / Enhanced Practice :: 从基础到挑战 || From Basics to Challenge

:::accordion
::panel { open: true }
### 基础题（3 题） || Basic (3 Questions)
> **Q1**
> α 粒子散射实验中，大多数 α 粒子直接穿过金箔说明了什么？ || In α scattering, most particles pass through. What does this tell us?

**📖 Q1 答案 || 📖 Q1 Answer**

原子内部大部分是空的。原子核极小，核外空间占原子体积的绑定大多数。α 粒子很少靠近核，所以大多不受影响地穿过。 || Most of the atom is empty space. The nucleus is tiny compared to the atom. Most α particles don't come close to the nucleus, so they pass through undeflected.

> **Q2**
> 写出镭-226 的 α 衰变方程（衰变后变为氡 Rn）。 || Write the α decay equation for Ra-226 (becomes Rn).

**📖 Q2 答案 || 📖 Q2 Answer**

$$
{}^{226}_{88}\text{Ra} \rightarrow {}^{222}_{86}\text{Rn} + {}^{4}_{2}\text{He}
$$

质量数守恒：226 = 222 + 4；电荷数守恒：88 = 86 + 2。 || Mass number: 226 = 222 + 4; charge: 88 = 86 + 2.

> **Q3**
> 某放射性元素半衰期 T=4 天，初始有 800 个原子核，经过 12 天后还剩多少？ || Half-life T=4 days, N₀=800. How many remain after 12 days?

**📖 Q3 答案 || 📖 Q3 Answer**

$$
N = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/T} = 800\times\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{12/4} = 800\times\frac{1}{8} = 100
$$

经过 3 个半衰期（12/4=3），剩余 100 个。 || After 3 half-lives (12/4=3), 100 remain.

::panel
### 进阶题（3 题） || Advanced (3 Questions)
> **Q4**
> 氘核（²H，2.014 u）和氚核（³H，3.016 u）聚变为氦核（⁴He，4.003 u）并放出一个中子（1.009 u）。求质量亏损和释放能量（1 u = 931.5 MeV）。 || D (2.014 u) + T (3.016 u) → He-4 (4.003 u) + n (1.009 u). Find mass defect and energy (1 u = 931.5 MeV).

**📖 Q4 答案 || 📖 Q4 Answer**

$$
\Delta m = (2.014 + 3.016) - (4.003 + 1.009) = 0.018\text{ u}
$$

$$
\Delta E = 0.018\times 931.5 = 16.77\text{ MeV}
$$

释放约 16.8 MeV 能量。这正是氢弹和未来聚变发电的能量来源。 || ~16.8 MeV released. This powers hydrogen bombs and future fusion reactors.

> **Q5**
> 某放射性元素的半衰期为 2 小时。初始有 N₀ 个原子核，经过多长时间后，只剩 N₀/32？ || Half-life = 2 hours. How long until only N₀/32 remain?

**📖 Q5 答案 || 📖 Q5 Answer**

$$
\frac{N_0}{32} = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/T} \implies \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^5 = \frac{1}{32}
$$

t/T = 5，t = 5 × 2 = 10 小时。经过 5 个半衰期。 || t/T = 5, t = 5 × 2 = 10 hours. After 5 half-lives.

> **Q6**
> 玻尔模型中，氢原子从 n=3 能级跃迁到 n=1，发出的光子波长是多少？（E₁=-13.6 eV，E₃=-1.51 eV，hc=1240 eV·nm） || Bohr model: H atom from n=3 to n=1. Find photon wavelength. (E₁=-13.6 eV, E₃=-1.51 eV, hc=1240 eV·nm)

**📖 Q6 答案 || 📖 Q6 Answer**

$$
\Delta E = E_3 - E_1 = -1.51 - (-13.6) = 12.09\text{ eV}
$$

$$
\lambda = \frac{hc}{\Delta E} = \frac{1240}{12.09} \approx 102.6\text{ nm}
$$

λ ≈ 102.6 nm，在紫外区（莱曼系）。 || λ ≈ 102.6 nm, in ultraviolet (Lyman series).
:::

## #redefine 01 / Overall Customer Journey :: 客户旅程：CRM的挑战与AI时代的机遇 || Customer Journey: CRM Challenges and Opportunities in the AI Era

CRM 要改善的是客户从需求、互动、承诺、履约、服务到续约或退出的完整体验，不只是某个模块的局部效率。[1][2] 六阶段总览标出 12 个常见问题及相应 Agentic CRM 机会；销售、客户服务、交付与现场服务三个后续愿景再把 24 个候选场景映射到各自业务旅程。每家企业仍需用自己的流程数据、客户反馈和风险要求重新评估。 || CRM must improve the complete customer experience from demand and engagement through commitment, delivery, service, renewal, or exit—not only the efficiency of one module.[1][2] The six-stage overview identifies 12 common problems and corresponding Agentic CRM opportunities. The later Sales, Customer Service, and Delivery and Field Service visions map the 24 candidate scenarios to their respective business journeys. Each enterprise must reassess them using its own process data, customer feedback, and risk requirements.

关系延续：续约 / 增长进入下一轮 || Relationship continues: renewal / growth enters the next cycle

关系结束：流失 / 退出，仅原因进入组织学习 || Relationship ends: churn / exit; only causes enter organizational learning

01

发现需求 || Discover Need

找到适合自身问题的方案 || Find an option that fits the problem

挑战 || Challenge

- 网站行为与市场活动脱离已知客户记录 || Web behavior and marketing activity remain separate from known customer records
- CRM 往往在线索形成后才看到需求 || CRM often sees demand only after a lead has formed

机遇 || Opportunity

- 汇集市场变化、数字行为、客户关系、产品使用与服务事件 || Combine market shifts, digital behavior, relationships, product use, and service events
- 识别需求形成、风险变化与最佳互动时点 || Identify emerging needs, changing risk, and the best engagement moment

02

互动识别 || Engage and Qualify

获得相关且连续的沟通 || Receive relevant and continuous engagement

挑战 || Challenge

- 线索评分依赖静态字段 || Lead scoring relies on static fields
- 营销转销售时，客户行为、互动历史与真实意图容易丢失 || Customer behavior, interaction history, and actual intent are often lost in the marketing-to-sales handoff

机遇 || Opportunity

- 围绕客户意图不断研究、问清、评估价值是否匹配 || Continuously research, clarify, and assess value fit around customer intent
- 只把有证据的变化转成互动或机会 || Turn only evidence-backed changes into engagement or opportunities

03

方案承诺 || Propose and Commit

得到透明且可兑现的承诺 || Receive a transparent, deliverable commitment

挑战 || Challenge

- 商机、报价、合同与交付能力分散 || Opportunities, quotes, contracts, and delivery capacity are fragmented
- 销售承诺难以在签署前完成跨团队校验 || Sales commitments are difficult to validate across teams before signature

机遇 || Opportunity

- 把需求、能力、价格、合同、风险和交付条件整理成一份可核实的承诺 || Organize needs, capability, pricing, contracts, risk, and delivery conditions into a verifiable commitment
- Agent 组织证据与协同；人负责关系、谈判和重大取舍 || Agents organize evidence and coordination; people own relationships, negotiation, and major trade-offs

04

履约采用 || Deliver and Adopt

尽快获得并采用预期价值 || Realize and adopt the expected value quickly

挑战 || Challenge

- 订单、项目、培训与产品使用数据分散 || Order, project, enablement, and product-usage data are fragmented
- CRM 能记录交付状态，却难以及时发现采用风险 || CRM records delivery status but rarely identifies adoption risk early

机遇 || Opportunity

- 连接订单、项目、供应链、培训与使用行为 || Connect orders, projects, supply chain, enablement, and usage behavior
- 让客户切实感受到承诺兑现的成效，并提前发现延期、范围和采用风险 || Turn commitments into customer-visible outcomes and surface delay, scope, and adoption risks early

05

服务恢复 || Serve and Recover

一次说明并解决问题 || Explain the issue once and get it resolved

挑战 || Challenge

- 渠道、案例、知识与现场工单相互断开 || Channels, cases, knowledge, and field work orders are disconnected
- 客户重复说明，远程客服与现场人员看到的背景不一致 || Customers repeat themselves while remote and field teams see different context

机遇 || Opportunity

- 连接意图识别、自助、带背景转人工、案例、知识与现场工单 || Connect intent detection, self-service, contextual human handoff, cases, knowledge, and field work orders
- 高风险、情感和异常场景由人员接管。[7][8][9] || People take over high-risk, emotional, and exceptional situations.[7][8][9]

06

关系走向 || Relationship Path

延续合作，或完成退出 || Continue the relationship or complete the exit

挑战 || Challenge

- 续约、扩展与退出判断依赖合同日期和个人经验 || Renewal, expansion, and exit decisions depend on contract dates and individual judgment
- 采用、服务成效、关系健康度、未完成承诺与流失原因未被整合 || Adoption, service outcomes, relationship health, open commitments, and churn causes remain disconnected

机遇 || Opportunity

- 整合采用、价值实现、关系健康度、服务历史与未完成承诺 || Combine adoption, value realization, relationship health, service history, and open commitments
- 形成关系延续或退出路径；推荐与倡导保持客户自愿 || Create a continuation or exit path while keeping referrals and advocacy explicitly voluntary

These categories are a diagnostic checklist rather than predetermined root causes. ## #capabilities 03 / Required Operating Capabilities :: 借助 Agent 修补运营断点，实现从数据、洞察到行动的闭环 || Use Agents to Repair Operating Breakpoints and Close the Loop from Data and Insight to Action

四类断点对应六项能力：感知事件、理解上下文、形成建议、协调人员与系统、在授权范围内执行、把结果用于改进。Agent 适用于需要理解非结构化信息、保持过程状态、跨系统调用工具、完成多步骤并动态处理异常的工作；规则处理固定条件，分析模型处理大样本预测，生成式 AI 处理单次内容生成，Agent 负责持续协调并推进到结果。是否允许 Agent 自动执行，由影响程度、是否可撤销、能否观察和实际运行数据决定。[10][11][12] || The four breakpoints require six capabilities: sense events, understand context, form recommendations, coordinate people and systems, act within authorization, and use outcomes for improvement. Agents fit work that requires interpreting unstructured information, maintaining process state, invoking tools across systems, completing multiple steps, and adapting to exceptions. Rules handle fixed conditions, analytical models handle large-sample prediction, generative AI handles one-time content generation, and agents coordinate and advance work toward an outcome. Impact, reversibility, observability, and operating evidence determine whether an agent may act automatically.[10][11][12]

01

感知 || Sense

持续接收业务事件、客户行为、邮件会议、服务会话与设备运行数据，并判断哪些变化值得进入当前客户流程或试点。稳定阈值用规则，复杂模式用分析模型。 || Continuously receive business events, customer behavior, email and meeting activity, service conversations, and equipment telemetry and determine which changes belong in the active customer process or pilot. Use rules for stable thresholds and analytical models for complex patterns.

02

理解 || Understand

把结构化记录、文档、对话、图像和历史关系组合成当前语境，区分事实、推断与未知项，并保留来源和权限。 || Combine structured records, documents, conversations, images, and relationship history into current context, distinguish facts, inferences, and unknowns, and retain sources and permissions.

03

决策 || Decide

根据业务目标、风险、客户偏好和资源约束，提出可选方案、优先级和下一步。高风险决定必须由确定性规则或人来批准。 || Use business goals, risk, customer preference, and resource constraints to generate alternatives, priorities, and next steps. High-risk decisions require deterministic rules or human approval.

04

协同 || Coordinate

让人、Agent、流程和系统一起推进，理顺输入输出、依赖、升级和异常，而不用用户去理解所有应用和接口。 || Coordinate people, agents, workflows, and systems in parallel, organizing inputs, outputs, dependencies, escalation, and exceptions without requiring users to understand every application and interface.

05

执行 || Act

在授权范围内更新记录、触发流程、生成材料、安排任务和沟通客户。可逆副作用使用幂等、回滚或补偿事务；不可逆或高影响动作在执行前使用预览、明确确认、审批或双人复核。 || Within authorized scope, update records, trigger workflows, create materials, schedule tasks, and communicate with customers. Reversible side effects use idempotency, rollback, or compensating transactions; irreversible or high-impact actions require preview, explicit confirmation, approval, or dual control before execution.

06

学习 || Learn

把接受、编辑、拒绝、结果和异常都反馈给流程、知识、模型和 Agent 评估，让整个循环随业务变化不断改进。 || Turn acceptances, edits, rejections, outcomes, and exceptions into feedback for workflows, knowledge, models, and agent evaluations so the cycle improves as the business changes.

学习结果回到记录、流程、知识、规则与评估，驱动下一轮感知 || Learning updates records, workflows, knowledge, rules, and evaluations for the next sensing cycle

学习如何受控 || How Learning Is Controlled

学习不等于模型自行训练。企业先验证业务结果和风险，再决定更新规则、流程、知识、评估或模型。 || Learning does not mean uncontrolled model retraining. The enterprise validates business outcomes and risk before changing rules, workflows, knowledge, evaluations, or models.

## #responsibility 04 / Human-Agent Work and Accountability :: Agent 持续推进，人掌握目标、取舍与责任 || Agents Advance the Work; People Retain Goals, Trade-Offs, and Accountability

人机协作的价值来自能力互补。Agent 负责持续感知、跨系统取数、证据整理、流程协调和任务跟进；人负责目标、客户关系、业务取舍、重大承诺以及例外与异常处理。对 106 项实验的综合分析显示，简单叠加人和 AI 并不保证更好：内容生成任务更容易获益，决策任务则需要清晰分工、确认点和责任边界。[10][11][12][13] 因此，每个场景先判断是否需要 Agent 持续推进有状态、多步骤、跨系统的工作，再确定哪些动作由 Agent 执行、哪些节点必须由人确认。所有分工方式下，人仍对目标和结果负责；系统记录来源、权限、确认点和升级路径。 || Human-Agent value comes from complementary capabilities. Agents continuously sense change, retrieve data across systems, organize evidence, coordinate workflows, and follow through on tasks. People own objectives, customer relationships, business trade-offs, material commitments, and exceptions. A meta-analysis of 106 experiments shows that simply combining people and AI does not guarantee better performance: content-creation tasks benefit more often, while decision tasks require clear work division, confirmation points, and accountability boundaries [10][11][12][13]. Each scenario should therefore first establish whether an agent must advance stateful, multi-step, cross-system work, then define which actions the agent performs and where people must confirm. People remain accountable for goals and outcomes in every pattern, while systems record sources, permissions, confirmation points, and escalation paths.

:::insight-grid { cols: 4, title: "Agent 与人的四种分工方式 || Four Ways Agents and People Divide Work" }
::card 洞察
### Agent 发现与解释，人判断 || Agents Find and Explain; People Judge
Agent 持续监测变化、汇集证据、解释原因并提出选项；人确认事实、业务语境和取舍。适用于需求变化、风险识别、客户研究和复杂分析。 || Agents monitor change, assemble evidence, explain causes, and propose options; people confirm facts, business context, and trade-offs. Use for demand change, risk detection, customer research, and complex analysis.

::card 起草
### Agent 起草，人确认 || Agents Draft; People Confirm
Agent 准备记录、内容、计划或下一步动作；人编辑、批准或拒绝后，系统才写入、发送或承诺。适用于高频且影响可控的业务动作。 || Agents prepare records, content, plans, or next actions; systems write, send, or commit only after people edit, approve, or reject. Use for frequent actions with controlled impact.

::card 协调
### Agent 协调推进，人处理取舍与例外 || Agents Coordinate; People Resolve Trade-Offs and Exceptions
Agent 保持流程状态，调用系统，组织依赖、提醒和升级；人处理资源冲突、客户关系、政策例外和重大取舍。适用于跨角色、多步骤流程。 || Agents maintain process state, invoke systems, and manage dependencies, reminders, and escalation; people resolve resource conflicts, relationships, policy exceptions, and major trade-offs. Use for cross-role, multi-step work.

::card 执行
### Agent 受控执行，人监督并负责 || Agents Act within Bounds; People Oversee and Remain Accountable
Agent 在金额、对象、时间和权限边界内执行可观察、可撤销的动作；异常、低置信和敏感情形升级。人监督结果，可以中断或回滚，并承担结果责任。 || Agents perform observable, reversible actions within amount, object, time, and permission boundaries and escalate exceptions, low confidence, and sensitive conditions. People oversee outcomes, can interrupt or roll back, and remain accountable.
:::

## #service-vision 07 / Agentic Customer Service Vision :: 客户服务从渠道和工单运营，转向一次说明、连续解决与持续学习 || Customer Service Moves from Channel and Case Operations to Explain-Once, Continuous Resolution, and Continuous Learning

客户服务愿景把跨渠道意图、自助、带上下文转人工、案例解决、知识和质量连成一条恢复链。Agent 处理边界明确的常规工作并保持上下文；座席和主管负责复杂判断、同理心、赔付、监管与服务质量。[7][8][9][24] 对 5,172 名客服的研究支持先做座席辅助和分组评估，不能直接推导全面自动化。[25] || The customer-service vision connects cross-channel intent, self-service, contextual human handoff, case resolution, knowledge, and quality into one recovery chain. ## #value 12 / Value Validation and ROI :: 以场景为单位验证客户成效、财务回报与扩展门槛 || Validate Customer Outcomes, Financial Return, and Scale Thresholds by Scenario

ROI 不能从 Agent 数量、生成内容量或行业平均值推导，而要从本文 24 个候选场景中选定一个可独立交付的客户流程，建立同口径基线和对照，验证一项客户或经营核心结果指标。随后再把实测增量换算为贡献毛利、释放产能或避免损失，并扣除建设、数据、许可、调用、人工确认、评估监控、变更、支持和补救成本。外部证据用于说明可观察的收益机制和参数量级；本企业的低、基准、高情景必须由自身基线、试点和真实报价建立。[32][33][34][35] || ROI cannot be inferred from agent count, generated-content volume, or industry averages. Select one independently deliverable customer process from the 24 candidate scenarios, establish a consistent baseline and comparison, and validate one primary customer or business outcome. Convert the measured increment into contribution margin, released capacity, or avoided loss, then subtract build, data, licensing, consumption, human confirmation, evaluation, monitoring, change, support, and remediation costs. External evidence identifies observable benefit mechanisms and plausible parameter orders of magnitude; enterprise low, base, and high cases must come from its own baseline, pilot, and actual quotes.[32][33][34][35]

### 销售：增量贡献毛利 || Sales: Incremental Contribution Margin

### 客服：直接 Agentic ROI 参考模型 || Service: A Direct Agentic ROI Reference Model
Salesforce 委托 Forrester 访谈 6 名 Agentforce 决策者，构造 50 名客服、首年 50 万案例的复合组织：三年风险调整后收益现值 280 万美元、成本现值 56.4 万美元、净现值 220 万美元、ROI 396%、一年内回收；模型假设案例自主分流率从 25% 升至 35%，转人工案例处理时间降低 50%。这是透明但受委托的复合模型，企业应替换全部输入。[33] || In a Salesforce-commissioned study, Forrester interviewed six Agentforce decision-makers and modeled a composite with 50 service representatives and 500,000 Year-1 cases. The three-year risk-adjusted model produced $2.8M benefit PV, $564K cost PV, $2.2M NPV, 396% ROI, and payback within one year; it assumed autonomous case deflection rising from 25% to 35% and a 50% handling-time reduction for transferred cases. This is a transparent but commissioned composite model; enterprises must replace every input.[33]

::card 03
### 现场服务：用于识别收益来源，不代表 Agent 增量 || Field Service: Identifies Benefit Sources, Not Agent Increment
Microsoft 委托 Forrester 访谈 7 家组织的 11 名代表，构造 1,000 名技术员、50 名调度员和 150 名管理者的复合组织；Dynamics 365 Field Service 三年模型为 346% ROI、3,310 万美元净现值、六个月内回收，并假设避免 12% 二次上门、技术员生产率最高提高 14%。该研究评估的是整体现场服务现代化，不是 Agentic AI 增量，因此只用于识别首次修复、技术员产能、开票和差旅等收益来源。[34] || In a Microsoft-commissioned study, Forrester interviewed 11 representatives at seven organizations and modeled a composite with 1,000 technicians, 50 dispatchers, and 150 managers. The three-year Dynamics 365 Field Service model produced 346% ROI, $33.1M NPV, and payback within six months, assuming 12% of repeat truck rolls avoided and technician productivity rising by up to 14%. The study evaluates overall field-service modernization rather than incremental agentic AI, so it identifies benefit sources such as first-time fix, technician capacity, invoicing, and travel only.[34]
:::

:::callout { title: "风险调整后的财务口径 || Risk-Adjusted Financial Definition" }
设互斥情景 `s` 的概率为 `p_s`，期间为 `t`，收益与成本分别为 `B_s,t` 和 `C_s,t`，折现率为 `r`，且 `Σp_s = 1`：`E(NPV)=Σ_s p_s Σ_t(B_s,t−C_s,t)/(1+r)^t`；`ROI=E(NPV)÷E(PV成本)`；回收期为累计风险调整后净现金流首次不小于 0 的月份。 || Let mutually exclusive scenario `s` have probability `p_s`, period `t`, benefit `B_s,t`, cost `C_s,t`, discount rate `r`, and `Σp_s = 1`: `E(NPV)=Σ_s p_s Σ_t(B_s,t−C_s,t)/(1+r)^t`; `ROI=E(NPV)÷E(PV cost)`; payback is the first month when cumulative risk-adjusted net cash flow is nonnegative.
:::

## #roadmap 13 / Enterprise Roadmap :: 投资门槛确定后，用四个阶段把一个场景交付并扩展 || After the Investment Gate, Deliver and Scale One Scenario in Four Phases

获得有上限的试点预算后，路线图不再重复计算 ROI，而是把已选场景转成可运行、可观察、可回滚的业务能力。团队先固化基线数据与责任，再重构规则、分析模型、Agent 和人的工作分工，并以影子模式或建议与草稿等低自治方式上线；随后只在已批准的成效、成本和底线指标内逐步扩展客户分组、角色、地区、工具与自治范围。[16][17][36] || After a capped pilot budget is approved, the roadmap does not recalculate ROI; it turns the selected scenario into an operable, observable, and reversible business capability. The team fixes baseline data and accountability, redesigns work across rules, analytical models, agents, and people, and launches in shadow mode or with low-autonomy recommendations and drafts. It then expands customer segments, roles, regions, tools, and autonomy only within approved outcome, cost, and guardrail thresholds.[16][17][36]

:::flow
1. **阶段 1 · 固化基线与责任 || Phase 1 · Fix the Baseline and Accountability** — 冻结试点客户分组、流程边界、一项核心结果指标、诊断指标、底线指标和成本口径；确认来源系统、业务负责人、人工接管、回滚与补偿路径。 || Freeze the pilot customer segment, process boundary, one primary outcome, diagnostic measures, guardrails, and cost definition; confirm source systems, business owner, human takeover, rollback, and compensating paths.
2. **阶段 2 · 重构人与 Agent 的工作 || Phase 2 · Redesign Human and Agent Work** — 重新排序步骤，删除无价值交接，明确规则、分析模型、Agent 和人的最佳分工，设计确认、升级和失败路径。 || Reorder steps, remove low-value handoffs, assign the right work to rules, analytical models, agents, and people, and design confirmation, escalation, and failure paths.
3. **阶段 3 · 影子运行与低自治上线 || Phase 3 · Shadow Run and Low-Autonomy Release** — 先在真实业务上只观察、不执行，比较 Agent 建议与人员结果；通过任务完成、工具选择、来源、延迟、成本与安全评估后，再开放建议、草稿和可逆动作。 || First observe on live work without taking action and compare agent recommendations with human outcomes. After task completion, tool choice, source, latency, cost, and safety evaluations pass, enable recommendations, drafts, and reversible actions.
4. **阶段 4 · 一次只扩一个维度 || Phase 4 · Expand One Dimension at a Time** — 将验证通过的身份、工具、知识、评估和界面纳入复用目录；每次只扩大客户分组、角色、地区、工具或自治中的一个维度，保留对照、监控和回滚，避免同时变化导致无法归因。 || Catalog validated identity, tools, knowledge, evaluations, and interfaces. Expand only one dimension at a time—customer segment, role, region, tool, or autonomy—while retaining comparison, monitoring, and rollback so simultaneous changes do not destroy attribution.
:::

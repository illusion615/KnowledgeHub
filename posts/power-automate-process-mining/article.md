## #methodology 01 / Methodology & Paradigms :: 挖掘方法论：三类范式选型与微软 HEAT 定位 || Methodology: Selecting Paradigms Within Microsoft HEAT

在微软企业级全栈自动化方法论（Holistic Enterprise Automation Techniques，简称 HEAT）的七个生命周期阶段（赋能、发现与规划、设计、构建与测试、部署与管理、安全与治理、培育）中，流程挖掘承担着“发现与规划”和“持续监控”两个核心锚点 [9]。与依赖人工填报的流程梳理不同，它直接解析业务数据库留存的时间戳轨迹，回答“真实路径长什么样”“耗时卡在哪个交接面”以及“哪条分支改造后净收益最高”三个工程问题 [1][2]。 || Within the seven stages of Microsoft's Holistic Enterprise Automation Techniques (HEAT)—Empower, Discover & Plan, Design, Build & Test, Deploy & Manage, Secure & Govern, and Nurture—process mining anchors both up-front discovery and post-launch drift monitoring [9]. Rather than relying on workshop recollections, it parses timestamped system traces to answer what path cases actually follow, where handoffs stall, and which interventions yield measurable returns [1][2].

在实际立项前，架构师首先需要根据业务跨度与数据源形态，在 Power Automate 提供的三种挖掘范式之间划清边界 [1][4]： || Before spinning up an environment, solution architects must match the business scope and underlying telemetry to one of three distinct mining paradigms in Power Automate [1][4]:

| 评估维度 || Dimension | 案例中心流程挖掘 (Case-Centric Process Mining) | 对象中心流程挖掘 (OCPM) | 任务挖掘 (Task Mining) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 分析粒度 || Granularity | 围绕单一业务主键（如发票号、工单号）串联端到端状态流转 [2] || End-to-end state transitions bound to a single business Case ID [2] | 追踪订单、发货单、发票与付款等多类业务对象的网状多对多交互 [4] || Many-to-many lifecycle web across Orders, Deliveries, Invoices, and Payments [4] | 捕获员工在桌面客户端跨窗口点击、复制粘贴与录入的微观键鼠动作 [1] || User desktop clicks, copy-pastes, and form entries across local apps [1] |
| 最小数据契约 || Minimum Schema | `Case ID`、`Activity Name`、`Start Timestamp`（活动日志另含 `End Timestamp`）[2] || `Case ID`, `Activity Name`, `Start Timestamp` (plus `End Timestamp` for activity logs) [2] | `Activity`、`Timestamp`、`Actor` 及每类对象独立列（同名多对象以 `|` 分隔，如 `O1|O2`）[4] || `Activity`, `Timestamp`, `Actor`, plus one column per object type (`|` delimiter such as `O1|O2`) [4] | Power Automate for desktop 录制器采集的操作步骤与屏幕上下文（需脱敏配置）[1] || Recorded UI actions and screen context captured via Power Automate for desktop [1] |
| 典型适用场景 || Ideal Scenarios | 请购审批、工单流转、患者入院登记、云端流 28 天运行历史体检 [1][2] || Approval routing, service tickets, patient intake, 28-day cloud flow run diagnostics [1][2] | 一张发票合并多笔订单、一次发运拆包履约等存在收敛与分叉假象的供应链流程 [4] || Order-to-Cash or Procure-to-Pay where split shipments and consolidated invoices distort single-ID logs [4] | 财务共享中心手工核对表格、遗留终端系统（如无接口客户端）的桌面流改造评估 [1][9] || Shared-service spreadsheet reconciliation and legacy thick-client RPA opportunity sizing [1][9] |
| 误用风险 || Misuse Pitfall | 强行把多对象业务压平为单主键，会制造虚假的重复环路与等待时长膨胀 [4] || Flattening multi-object events into one Case ID fabricates false loops and inflated wait times [4] | 将本可用单主键表达的简单审批强建多对象表，徒增数据湖清洗负担 [4] || Over-modeling simple single-ticket workflows increases lakehouse ETL overhead unnecessarily [4] | 在跨部门审批尚未理顺前过早录制桌面动作，容易把本应淘汰的冗余步骤自动化 [1][9] || Recording desktop tasks before fixing upstream process handoffs automates wasteful steps [1][9] |

在微软自动化成熟度模型（Level 100 初始探索至 Level 500 高效自治）中，团队通常在 Level 200（可重复阶段）引入标准模板完成首个试点发现，在 Level 300（已定义阶段）由自动化卓越中心（Automation CoE）沉淀统一的事件日志规范，并在 Level 400–500 阶段打通规则告警与智能体闭环 [9][10]。 || Across Microsoft's Automation Maturity Model (Level 100 Initial through Level 500 Optimizing), organizations typically adopt prebuilt templates for first-wave discovery at Level 200, standardize event-log contracts under an Automation CoE at Level 300, and wire automated rule remediation at Levels 400–500 [9][10].

:::callout { title: "方法论铁律：先用系统级流程挖掘定航向，再用桌面级任务挖掘做切片 || Methodology Rule: Anchor Macro Bottlenecks Before Recording Desktop Tasks" }
端到端跨部门流程应始终先运行流程挖掘，定位延迟最重或返工率最高的两个活动节点；仅当瓶颈节点确属人工在老旧界面间高频搬运数据时，才对该岗位定向开启任务挖掘，避免全员漫灌式录制带来隐私合规阻力与噪声数据 [1][9]。 || Always run system-level process mining first to isolate the specific handoffs driving cycle-time delays or rework; deploy desktop task mining surgically on those bottleneck activities only when human UI manipulation is the root cause, avoiding broad, noisy screen recording [1][9].
:::

## #project-lifecycle 02 / End-to-End Project Lifecycle :: 项目流程：企业级流程挖掘六阶段闭环 || Project Lifecycle: The Six-Stage Enterprise Loop

一个可复用的 Power Automate Process Mining 项目并非“上传一份表格看流程图”的一次性演示，而是贯穿价值立项、数据工程、深度归因、仿真验证、自动化改造与生产运维的闭环工程 [2][6][9]。以下六个阶段提炼自 Microsoft Learn 官方实施路径与 CoE 治理指南 [2][7][9]： || A production-grade Power Automate Process Mining engagement is not a one-off CSV upload demo; it is a closed engineering loop spanning value scoping, data pipelines, root-cause proof, simulation, action wiring, and ALM operations [2][6][9]. The six stages below synthesize the official Microsoft Learn implementation sequence and CoE governance guidance [2][7][9]:

:::flow { title: "Power Automate Process Mining 六阶段项目交付闭环 || Six-Stage Delivery Loop for Power Automate Process Mining" }
1. **阶段 1：价值界定与双角色立项 || Stage 1: Value Framing & CoE Backlog Intake** — 由业务负责人与 CoE 共同锁定起止里程碑（如从“采购申请提交”到“发票完成清账”），借助 GitHub 开源 `Automation Project` 应用登记预期工时节省与合规目标，并在目标环境中核验 `Environment Maker` 权限与存储配额 [9][11][12]。 || Business owners and the CoE lock exact start/end boundary events (for instance, requisition submitted to invoice cleared), log baseline KPIs in the `Automation Project` intake app, and verify `Environment Maker` roles and storage entitlements [9][11][12].
2. **阶段 2：事件日志抽取与模式映射 || Stage 2: Event Log Extraction & Schema Mapping** — 区分仅含单时间戳的“事件日志”与含起止双时间戳的“活动日志”，通过数据湖直连、Power Query 连接器或预置 ERP 模板接入源表，并利用摄取阶段的 Copilot 自动推荐必填列与财务、资源等扩展属性映射 [2][3][5]。 || Distinguish single-timestamp event logs from start/end activity logs, connect via OneLake/ADLS Gen2 or Power Query templates, and use Copilot in ingestion to automap mandatory keys plus financial and resource attributes [2][3][5].
3. **阶段 3：变体收敛、合规比对与根因诊断 || Stage 3: Variant Triage, BPMN Conformance & Root Cause Analysis** — 在 Web 端流程智能工作区巡检核心指标卡片后，转入 Process Mining 桌面应用拆解主流变体与长尾异常，导入标准 BPMN 蓝图做图层合规比对，并用根因分析（RCA）决策树定位导致超时的关键案例属性 [5][6]。 || Triage headline KPIs in the Web Process Intelligence workspace, then open the Process Mining desktop app to isolate tail variants, overlay target BPMN diagrams for conformance checks, and run Root Cause Analysis (RCA) decision trees [5][6].
4. **阶段 4：返工剥离与流程变更仿真 || Stage 4: Rework Isolation & Process Simulation** — 量化单节点自循环（Self-loop）与跨节点往返环路（Loop）造成的隐性成本，在桌面应用的仿真引擎中调整资源配比、案件到达率或活动耗时，在零生产干扰的快照上验证改进假设的吞吐收益 [6]。 || Quantify self-loops and multi-step rework loops, then configure ML-backed simulation scenarios in the desktop app—adjusting staffing, arrival rates, or step durations on an isolated snapshot before touching production [6].
5. **阶段 5：触发自动化干预与智能体协同 || Stage 5: Triggered Automation & Agentic Analytics** — 将验证有效的改进点落地为云端流或非值守桌面流；对关键指标配置案例级业务规则（Case-scope Business Rules），每次数据刷新后自动触发云端流催办或纠偏，并通过 MCP 服务端接入 Copilot Studio 对话式分析助手 [7][8]。 || Implement approved automations via Cloud or Desktop Flows, bind case-scope business rules so every data refresh triggers corrective flows automatically, and expose analytics to business stakeholders through the Process Mining MCP server in Copilot Studio [7][8].
6. **阶段 6：增量刷新、解决方案晋升与持续度准 || Stage 6: Incremental Refresh, Solution ALM & Drift Monitoring** — 配置每日定时增量刷新以控制数据吞吐成本，通过 Power Platform 解决方案与 Power BI 部署管道将流程实体与定制报表从开发态迁移重绑至生产态，持续监控优化前后的周期收敛度 [2][7]。 || Schedule daily incremental refreshes, promote process entities and custom Power BI reports across Dev/Test/Prod using Power Platform solutions and workspace rebinding, and track post-automation KPI drift [2][7].
:::

### 数据准备阶段的七类属性映射准则 || Seven Attribute Mapping Rules During Data Preparation

许多首次落地项目在第三阶段做不出根因结论，病根往往出在第二阶段只映射了三个必填字段 [2]。官方文档将数据模型划分为三个核心必填项与六类分析扩展属性，映射时需严格区分“事件级”与“案例级”解释机制 [2]： || Many first-time projects fail to surface actionable root causes because teams map only the three mandatory columns during ingestion [2]. Microsoft Learn defines three required fields alongside specialized optional attribute types whose aggregation semantics differ between event-level and case-level operations [2]:

- **三个刚性必填字段**：`Case ID`（贯穿同一流程实例的唯一标识，每条记录均不可为空）、`Activity Name`（描述具体业务动作的动词短语，如 `Submit Request` 或 `Approve Invoice`）以及 `Start Timestamp`（事件发生或活动开始的精确时间戳；若源表同时提供 `End Timestamp`，系统才能精确拆分“主动处理时长”与“节点间排队等待时长”）[2]。 || **Three mandatory columns**: `Case ID` (non-null instance identifier across all steps), `Activity Name` (clear action label), and `Start Timestamp` (adding `End Timestamp` unlocks exact separation between active handling time and inter-step waiting time) [2].
- **案例级属性的首末事件解释机制**：诸如发票总额、客户等级或申请部门等每个案例理应唯一的值，在增量日志中可能随行变化。映射为 `Case Level Attribute (first event)` 或 `Financial Per Case (first event)` 时，系统在完整保留原始行数据的同时，做案例级过滤或根因相关性计算会统一采信时间最早那条事件的值；若业务以终审金额为准，则应选 `last event` 口径 [2][8]。 || **First-event vs. last-event case attribute resolution**: Attributes like invoice total or customer tier may vary across incremental log rows. Mapping as `Case Level Attribute (first event)` or `Financial Per Case (first event)` preserves raw event values while instructing case-level filters and correlation engines to evaluate the chronologically first (or `last`) event value consistently [2][8].
- **动作级成本与执行资源**：将快递费、人工单次审核成本映射为 `Financial per Event`（支持按总和、均值、极值聚合），并将经办人或系统机器人账号映射为 `Resource`，才能在桌面端生成部门协作社交网络图与资源负载仿真 [2][6]。 || **Per-event financials and resource actors**: Mapping per-step fees to `Financial per Event` and human or bot executors to `Resource` unlocks organizational social charts, handover bottleneck matrices, and staffing simulations in the desktop app [2][6].

### 桌面端根因分析与返工诊断实操要点 || Desktop App Root Cause Analysis & Rework Diagnostics

Web 端提供的卡片式“流程智能（Process Intelligence）”工作区适合日常多标签页看板盯盘与动态筛选，而深水区的结构化归因则必须依赖 **Power Automate Process Mining 桌面应用**（原 Minit 企业级分析引擎，持 Power Automate Premium 许可即可解锁）[5][6]： || While the Web-based Process Intelligence workspace excels at customizable multi-tab dashboards and cross-card filtering, deep structural diagnostics rely on the **Power Automate Process Mining desktop app** (unlocked with a Power Automate Premium license) [5][6]:

- **返工三指标拆解**：桌面端统计屏与流程图图层将重复劳动拆解为三项精确计数——`Self-loop`（同一活动紧接着自身重复执行，如连续九次修改同一凭证）、`Loop`（至少经过一个中间活动后折返，如“设定交期 → 审批驳回”反复七次）以及二者的总和 `Rework` [6]。优先消除高频 `Loop` 往往比单纯加速单个节点更能压缩总周期 [6]。 || **Three-tier rework decomposition**: The desktop app separates repetitions into `Self-loop` (an activity immediately repeating itself), `Loop` (revisiting an activity after one or more intermediate steps, such as repeated approval rejections), and total `Rework` [6]. Eliminating multi-step loops typically yields larger cycle-time reductions than speeding up isolated tasks [6].
- **根因决策树（RCA）防自相关陷阱**：在运行根因分析定位超时元凶时，官方明确警示**切勿在 `Influenced by` 候选因子中勾选与目标指标同源的派生指标**（例如目标选 `Case duration` 时，绝不能勾选 `Case Active Time`），否则决策树每一次分裂都会被同义时间列霸占，掩盖真正导致延误的供应商类别、地区或审批通道属性 [6]。 || **Avoiding tautological splits in Root Cause Analysis (RCA)**: When configuring RCA decision trees, never include collinear time metrics in the `Influenced by` list—for example, exclude `Case Active Time` when targeting `Case duration`—otherwise the algorithm splits on the proxy metric at every branch instead of surfacing business drivers like vendor tier or region [6].
- **层级化挖掘（Hierarchical Process Mining）**：面对上百个细碎活动组成的意大利面条式流程图，可通过聚类属性定义多层目录结构，但必须满足**一对一确定性归属前提**（每个具体 `Activity` 或 `Resource` 只能唯一归属一个上级聚类值，或留空置于簇外），否则系统校验将拦截保存 [6]。 || **Hierarchical process mining**: To tame "spaghetti" maps containing dozens of granular events, group activities or resources into multi-level clusters—ensuring strict many-to-one mapping where each `Activity` or `Resource` maps to at most one parent cluster value [6].

:::insight-grid { cols: 2, title: "四组高频微软工具链黄金搭配 || Four High-Value Microsoft Tooling Combinations" }

### 大体量日志：Fabric OneLake / ADLS Gen2 + 星型模式 JSON || High-Volume Logs: Fabric OneLake / ADLS Gen2 + Normalized Star Schema
当事件规模超出轻量文件上传上限时，应跳过中间数据流转换，直接连接开启分层命名空间的 **Azure Data Lake Storage Gen2** 或 **Fabric Lakehouse** 读取 Delta 表、物化视图或同构 Parquet 文件夹 [3]。对于包含一张事件表、可选案例表与多张维表的星型数据模型，可通过预览版规范化模式（Normalized Schema）编写一份 JSON 配置文件声明存储后端与字段映射，实现零冗余宽表拼装直连摄取 [3]。 || For enterprise-scale logs, bypass intermediate ETL by reading Delta tables, materialized views, or Parquet folders directly from hierarchical-namespace **ADLS Gen2** or **Fabric OneLake** [3]. When source data sits in a star schema (event table plus case and lookup tables), supply a Normalized Schema JSON manifest so Process Mining ingests tables directly without pre-flattening wide files [3].

::card 02
### 核心业务套件：SAP ERP（四表模板 / RFC）与 Dataverse OData || Core Systems: SAP ERP (4-Table Template / RFC) & Dataverse OData
针对 SAP 应付账款等经典场景，优先启用内置财务与运营模板，通过 SQL Server、Oracle 或 OData 读取保留原名的四张核心表（凭证抬头 `BKPF`、凭证行项目 `BSEG`、变更抬头 `CDHDR`、变更明细 `CDPOS`），亦可经本地数据网关调用预览版 SAP ERP 连接器执行 RFC/BAPI 抽取 [3][5]。连接 **Microsoft Dataverse**（如提取 `activitypointers` 活动表）或已有数据流时需特别注意：平台不支持原生 Dataverse 连接器与 Dataflow 连接器，必须改选 **OData 连接器**并在环境地址末尾拼接 `api/data/v9.2` 完成组织账户认证 [2]。 || For SAP Accounts Payable, use the Finance & Operations template against canonical tables (`BKPF`, `BSEG`, `CDHDR`, `CDPOS`) or extract via the SAP ERP RFC/BAPI connector over an on-premises data gateway [3][5]. When ingesting from **Microsoft Dataverse** (such as `activitypointers`), note that native Dataverse and Dataflow connectors are unsupported—connect via the **OData** connector appending `api/data/v9.2` to the environment URL [2].

## #best-practices 05 / Production Best Practices :: 最佳实践：许可容量、数据治理与避坑清单 || Best Practices: Sizing, Governance & Pitfalls

将流程挖掘从单次咨询试点推向企业常态化运营，往往会在容量计费、云存储跨域权限、定时刷新频率与人员离职交接四个环节踩坑 [2][3][11]。以下最佳实践均锚定微软官方许可指南与排障手册的硬性指标 [2][3][7][11]： || Scaling process mining from an initial proof-of-value into steady-state operations requires navigating strict platform guardrails around storage capacity, cross-origin lake permissions, refresh cadences, and ownership continuity [2][3][7][11]:

### 1. 许可与容量池规划：算清用户赠送额度与 100 GB 扩容包边界 || 1. Licensing & Capacity Sizing: Balance Per-User Entitlements Against Capacity Add-Ons

每位持有 **Power Automate Premium**（目录参考价 `$15/用户/月`，年付）的用户除具备有人值守桌面流与云端流权限外，自动向租户池贡献 **50 MB** Process Mining 数据存储额度，租户级免费累计上限为 **100 GB** [11]。当历史事件日志体量突破免费池或需要挂载大型 ERP 明细时，企业可叠加采购 **Process Mining 容量附加包**（`$5,000/100 GB 存储数据/月`，前置条件为具备 Power Automate Premium，每个附加包同步附带 `2 GB` Dataverse 数据库容量、`1 TB` 文件容量及每 24 小时 `250,000` 次 Power Platform 请求配额）[11]。若后续改进方案涉及无人值守后台机器人执行，则需按并发机器数配置 **Power Automate Process**（`$150/机器人/月`）或微软托管虚拟机 **Hosted Process**（`$215/机器人/月`）容量许可 [11]。 || Each **Power Automate Premium** license (`$15/user/month`, billed annually) unlocks full Process Mining desktop and web capabilities and contributes **50 MB** of Process Mining storage to the tenant pool (capped at **100 GB** per tenant) [11]. Enterprise logs exceeding pooled user entitlements add **Process Mining Storage** capacity packs (`$5,000 per 100 GB/month`, which also include `2 GB` Dataverse DB, `1 TB` File capacity, and `250,000` Power Platform Requests/24h) [11]. Downstream unattended RPA remediation requires **Power Automate Process** (`$150/bot/month`) or **Hosted Process** (`$215/bot/month`) capacity [11].

### 2. 数据湖直连与安全配置：严守 Gen2 命名空间、CORS 域名与服务主体最小权限 || 2. Lakehouse Security: Enforce Hierarchical Namespace, CORS Origins, and Service Principal Roles

采用自带 **ADLS Gen2** 直连时，存储账户必须为 Gen2 且开启**分层命名空间（Hierarchical Namespace）**；首次绑定容器的操作人需具备存储账户级 `Owner` 角色，而所有流程共同所有者（Co-owner）至少需具备 `Storage Blob Data Reader`（或 `Contributor`）加 `Azure Resource Manager Reader` 角色，同时必须在存储账户 CORS 规则中放行 `https://make.*.powerautomate.com` 与 `https://make.*.powerapps.com` 两个跨域源 [3]。若采用 **Fabric OneLake** 直连，切勿使用个人默认的 `My workspace`，必须在租户设置中开启允许外部应用访问 OneLake 数据，并将工作区共享给官方 `Process Insight Prod` 服务主体 [3]。 || For **BYO ADLS Gen2**, enable **Hierarchical Namespace** (Gen1 is unsupported), assign storage-account `Owner` for initial setup plus `Storage Blob Data Reader` and `ARM Reader` for co-owners, and whitelist `https://make.*.powerautomate.com` and `https://make.*.powerapps.com` in CORS [3]. For **Fabric OneLake**, never use `My workspace`—use a dedicated workspace, enable external app OneLake access in tenant settings, and share the workspace with the `Process Insight Prod` service principal [3].

### 3. Power Query 清洗、定时刷新与 14 天休眠重算机制 || 3. Power Query Hygiene, Daily Incremental Refresh, and the 14-Day Inactivity Rule

在内置 Power Query 编辑器修改已有映射时，**严禁在工具栏重复点击“选择列（Choose columns）”等已存在于“应用的步骤（Applied steps）”列表中的操作**，否则新步骤会追加在流水线末尾并丢失前序已被筛除的候选字段，正确做法是双击既有步骤原地编辑 [2]。在运维期，平台定时刷新的最高频率为**每 1 天一次**；针对千万级事件表务必启用增量刷新（Incremental Refresh）仅拉取新增行 [2]。此外要注意两项时效约束：若连续 **14 天**未打开某个流程的分析页，再次访问时必须点击重新分析（Re-analyze）激活视图；而针对云端流自身运行历史的流程洞察仅覆盖最近 **28 天**的运行记录（新运行产生后约需 15–30 分钟入库，且不支持 Teams 内置 Dataverse 环境）[5][7]。 || When editing Power Query steps, **never click `Choose columns` again on the toolbar if it already exists in `Applied steps`**—edit the existing step in place so previously filtered columns remain selectable [2]. Scheduled refresh runs at most **once every 1 day**, making incremental refresh essential for large tables [2]. Keep two platform timers in mind: analytics views unvisited for **14 days** require a quick re-analyze pass on next open, and cloud-flow Process Insights covers runs from the past **28 days** with a 15–30 minute ingestion lag (excluding Dataverse for Teams environments) [5][7].

### 4. 协作共享、ALM 重绑与流程所有者离职兜底 || 4. Sharing Roles, ALM Workspace Rebinding, and Owner Offboarding Continuity

新建流程默认仅创建者本人可见，对外共享仅开放两种颗粒度：**共同所有者（Co-owner）**可编辑配置、刷新数据并继续向外共享，而**查看者（Viewer）**仅可浏览流程详情与分析看板 [7]。需要警惕的治理盲区是：**当原流程所有者（Owner）离职且账号被禁用时，共同所有者不会自动晋升为新任所有者**，管理员必须进入 Power Platform 管理中心（PPAC）将该员工名下的 Dataverse 流程记录显式重新指派给继任者 [7]。在跨环境发布时，切勿在生产环境手工重配连接与自定义指标；应将流程实体纳入 Power Platform 解决方案配合 `microsoft/powerplatform-actions` 自动导入，收到目标环境首页橙色感叹号提示后完成连接绑定，并对自定义 Power BI 报表执行管道迁移与数据集重绑（Rebinding）[7][14]。 || Process sharing offers two roles: **Co-owner** (can edit, refresh, and reshare) and **Viewer** (read-only analytics) [7]. Critically, **disabling a departing Owner's Entra account does not promote a Co-owner automatically**—administrators must reassign the user's Dataverse process records in PPAC [7]. For ALM, package process entities, custom metrics, and business rules inside Power Platform solutions promoted via `microsoft/powerplatform-actions`, complete post-import connection setup, and use Power BI pipelines with dataset rebinding rather than rebuilding reports in production [7][14].
